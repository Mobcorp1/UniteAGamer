# UAG Commercial Economy Lock — 29 September 2026

This document is the commercial source of truth for the closed-beta monetisation pass. Values remain admin/configurable where practical, but implementation must not silently drift from this model.

## Core plans

| Tier | Monthly | Annual | Trade actions | Match Raider searches | Ads |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Core Raider / Free | £0 | £0 | 5 / month | 5 / month | Full eligible ad model + optional rewarded |
| Active Raider / Essential | £4.99 | £49.99 | 30 / month | 30 / month | Reduced passive banner only |
| Elite Raider / Premium | £8.99 | £89.99 | Unlimited | Unlimited | None |

Trade and Match Raider are monthly core allowances. Existing weekly limits for unrelated systems remain separate unless explicitly migrated.

## Raider Marks

- Free tier only.
- One verified completed rewarded ad earns one Raider Mark.
- Five Raider Marks redeem for one bonus action.
- The user chooses either +1 Trade Action or +1 Match Raider Search.
- Wallet cap: 10 Marks.
- Rewarded-ad cap: 3/day and 20/month.
- Redemption cap: 4 bonus actions/month.
- Minimum interval: 20 minutes.
- Marks carry over; reward redemptions do not upgrade Match Intelligence depth or any paid entitlement.
- Wallet, caps and reward grants must be server-authoritative before production rewarded inventory is enabled.

Production rewarded delivery remains blocked until the real AdMob rewarded unit, UMP consent flow and server-side verification/grant path are complete.

## Refer a Raider

First qualifying paid purchase receives 10% off.

Recurring referrer commission is calculated from eligible net collected subscription revenue after discount/payment costs and remains subject to validation, refunds and chargebacks:

- 1–4 active paid referrals: 5%
- 5–24: 7.5%
- 25–49: 10%
- 50–99: 12.5%
- 100+: 15%
- active Premium referrer: +2.5 percentage points

Commission validation: 30 days. Payout threshold: £25.

Existing referral milestones remain:
- 1 validated referral: 7 days Premium
- 3: 30 days Premium
- 5: Creator Programme fast-track review

Self-referral is prohibited.

## Creator acquisition

- Standard approved creator code: 20% off first qualifying paid purchase.
- Selected creator campaign: 25% off first qualifying paid purchase.
- Exceptional 50% creator campaign: no cash commission on that discounted transaction.
- Acquisition discounts are one-time, not lifetime.
- Creator commissions follow the existing 5%→20% points ladder plus approved community uplift.
- Free creator giveaway periods do not earn commission; a later genuine paid conversion may.

Approved creators retain the existing discounted own Premium benefit, aligned to £6.99/month at launch.

## Owner/admin campaigns

Owner campaigns are server-validated, new-customer acquisition offers and never stack.

Default presets:
- 20% off first month: 100 uses.
- 25% off first month: 50 uses.
- 50% off first month: 25 uses; no commission.
- Christmas: join on a qualifying monthly plan and the next monthly renewal is free; no commission is generated by the free invoice.

Admin can set public code, validity window and redemption cap, and deactivate immediately.

## Founding Premium

Founding Raider Premium Annual launches at £44.99/year (50% of the £89.99 launch annual price), capped to 100 Founder memberships.

The Founder rate requires continuous subscription. Voluntary cancellation forfeits the Founder rate; returning customers pay the then-current public price. Normal involuntary payment recovery/grace must not be treated as voluntary forfeiture.

## Gift Premium

Gift a Month of Premium:
- price: £6.99 one-off
- duration: 30 days
- no auto-renewal
- purchaser cannot redeem their own gift
- recipient must be Free/lapsed with no active paid, pass or temporary Premium access
- claim window: 30 days from purchase
- one discounted Premium gift redemption per recipient per rolling 365 days
- sender cap: 5 paid gifts/month
- gift purchase earns no creator/referral commission
- gift does not stack with another temporary Premium entitlement
- later genuine paid conversion may use normal referral attribution rules

Gift purchase and redemption are server-authoritative.

## Discount integrity

- One promotion per purchase.
- Explicit invalid codes fail checkout instead of silently charging full price.
- Discount percentages, eligibility, redemption counts and commission eligibility are trusted only from server/admin records.
- Paid-history users cannot repeatedly reuse first-purchase acquisition offers.
- Promotion claims are recorded server-side.
- Refunds/chargebacks can reverse commissions and rewards.
- Client-side state never authorises a commercial discount or entitlement.

## Business objective

The operating objective is at least £3,000/month profit, with roughly £5,000/month platform contribution used as the healthier planning target. Discounting is an acquisition tool rather than the default route to the target.

Closed-beta measurement should replace modelling assumptions with actual:
- Free MAU and ad yield
- rewarded opt-in/completion/eCPM
- Free→Essential and Free→Premium conversion
- Essential→Premium conversion
- 30/60/90-day paid retention and churn
- creator/referral CAC-equivalent cost
- gift→paid conversion
- net contribution after discounts, commissions, payment/store costs, refunds and infrastructure
