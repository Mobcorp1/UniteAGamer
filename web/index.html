FROM COLLECTION TO ACTION

One collection.
A more useful plan.

Learning a blueprint and finding another copy are different events. If you have learned a blueprint and hold two extra copies, record it as Owned with Dupes Owned: 2. That keeps your missing list useful and makes your spare copies easier to identify when considering a trade.

Use Raid Planner to focus on what you still need. Its regional conditions come from the official ARC Raiders map-conditions source; community drop reports add player observations. Treat those observations as clues when choosing a target, rather than promises that a particular item will appear.

Once you have a goal, Favourite Loadout gives you a place to prepare your equipment. Check the selected weapon’s compatible attachments and each quick-use slot. A prepared loadout is a planning record: you still need to equip those items in ARC Raiders before entering a raid.

The guides explain these distinctions with examples, including how to review a photo import and how to agree a trade without giving away account credentials.

WHAT TO EXPECT

A companion for your own decisions

UAG records the information you enter and helps you organise it. It does not read your game account, equip items in the game or guarantee loot or trades. Check your current in-game inventory when correcting a record, and check the official conditions when an event time matters.
