Who is responsible for your data

MobCorp Limited, 107 Langley Hall Road, Solihull, B92 7HD, United Kingdom, operates UAG ARC Raiders Hub. Privacy and data-rights enquiries can be sent to contact@mobcorp.co.uk.

Data we may process

Depending on the features you use, this can include account email, display/Raider name, region, platform, timezone, age confirmation, consent records, profile preferences, availability, blueprint ownership and duplicates, loadouts, listings, offers, trade history, reputation signals, reports, community intel, moderation decisions, referral and creator-programme records, ad/consent events, subscription tier, entitlement state, provider-confirmed payment events, device/application diagnostics and support communications.

Why we use it

We use data to provide requested account and companion features, perform our contract with users, secure and administer the service, prevent fraud and abuse, moderate community activity, operate subscriptions and entitlements, respond to support, maintain records required by law, and pursue legitimate interests such as service security, analytics and product improvement where those interests are not overridden by individual rights. Where consent is required for a specific activity, we will ask for it separately.

Firebase, analytics, ads and other providers

Google/Firebase services may support authentication, databases, hosting, messaging, analytics and operational infrastructure. Advertising services may process ad-request and consent information where ads are enabled. Payment providers process payment information; UAG should not store full card or bank credentials.

Moderation and OCR

When enabled for beta or production, safety-sensitive text may be processed using Google Cloud Natural Language to support moderation. Images submitted to OCR-enabled features may be processed using Google Cloud Vision. Cloud OCR is separate from the existing Blueprint recognition/scanner engine. Provider use will be limited to the information reasonably necessary for the requested or safety function.

Sharing and international transfers

Data may be shared with service providers that help operate authentication, hosting, databases, communications, analytics, advertising, payments, security, moderation, OCR and support, and with professional advisers, regulators or authorities where required. Some providers may process data outside the UK; where required, an applicable adequacy decision, approved contractual safeguard or other lawful transfer mechanism will be used.

Retention

Personal data is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, the purpose collected, security, fraud prevention, disputes, legal obligations and the establishment or defence of legal claims. Different records can require different retention periods.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, and rights concerning certain automated decisions. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Some rights are subject to legal exemptions. Contact contact@mobcorp.co.uk to make a request.

Complaints

Please contact MobCorp Limited first so a concern can be investigated. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office about the handling of personal data.

Changes

UAG is still developing. We may update this policy when features, providers, laws or data practices change. Material changes will be communicated where required.

Beta legal review

This is the operational policy for the beta service. MobCorp intends to obtain qualified UK legal review before general public launch, particularly for subscriptions, international availability, privacy processing and third-party game intellectual property.
