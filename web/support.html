What to include

Include the email address on your UAG account, a short description of the issue and any relevant order, listing, offer, report or notification reference. Screenshots can help where appropriate.

Do not send

Do not send passwords, one-time authentication codes, full payment-card numbers, private signing keys or other authentication secrets.

Billing support

For missing entitlement, cancellation or refund questions, identify whether the purchase was made through Google Play or the UAG web/Stripe route and include the relevant provider order/reference number where safe to do so.

Privacy and data rights

Use contact@mobcorp.co.uk for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or other privacy requests. We may need to verify account ownership before acting on a request.

Account deletion

To request deletion of your UAG account and associated personal data, use the in-app account deletion route when available or email contact@mobcorp.co.uk with the subject Account deletion. We may verify account ownership. Cancelling an active Google Play or Stripe subscription is a separate step and should be completed through the relevant billing provider. Data that must be retained for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention or disputes may be retained only for the necessary period.
