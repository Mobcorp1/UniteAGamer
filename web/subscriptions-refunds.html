Subscriptions & Refunds

Effective 18 September 2026 · MobCorp Limited

How UAG handles billing authority, renewals, cancellation and refund requests.

TermsPrivacySupport

Android purchases

Paid digital access purchased inside the Google Play-distributed Android app is intended to use Google Play Billing. Google Play controls checkout, payment collection and subscription-management records. UAG grants entitlement only after provider-confirmed purchase state is available.

Web purchases

Paid digital access purchased through the UAG web service may use Stripe Checkout and the Stripe Customer Portal. Stripe processes supported payment credentials; UAG should retain only the provider references and entitlement information needed to operate the account.

Renewal and cancellation

Recurring subscriptions continue until cancelled. The checkout surface must show the price, billing period and material renewal terms before purchase. Google Play subscriptions should be managed through Google Play subscription controls. Stripe web subscriptions should be managed through the Stripe Customer Portal when enabled. If you cannot access the correct cancellation route, contact contact@mobcorp.co.uk.

Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on the payment route, purchase circumstances, provider rules and applicable law. Google Play purchases may be requested through Google Play and may also be reviewed by MobCorp Limited where the developer can assist. Stripe web refund requests should be sent to contact@mobcorp.co.uk. Nothing in this policy removes statutory consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Refunds, revocations and chargebacks

If a payment is refunded, revoked, charged back or otherwise reversed, the related paid entitlement, reward or commission may be removed or recalculated once the provider-confirmed event is processed.

Beta, founder and pass pricing

UAG may offer beta, founder, pass or promotional pricing. The price and conditions accepted at checkout apply to that transaction or subscription subject to applicable law. Material changes to an existing recurring paid service will be communicated as required.

Compliance assurance

This billing framework is maintained through UAG's internal UK compliance self-audit. Independent professional UK legal review is planned once UAG begins generating revenue or before material commercial or international scale. Google Play subscription product IDs, cancellation links and final server-side billing verification still need to be completed before accepting Android subscription payments.

Billing support: contact@mobcorp.co.uk
