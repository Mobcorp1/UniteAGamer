Track progress at the correct workshop

Bench Tracker provides a place to review resource requirements and keep track of upgrades. A completed bench may be hidden from the Command Centre’s upgrade carousel while remaining available in Bench Tracker. The carousel is a summary, not the authoritative list of every bench.

Research Station progression

Research Station levels I–IV require materials associated with the Outpost expansion. The current app contains the reference requirements, but Outpost completion is not yet fully persisted or prerequisite-enforced. Check the Research Station materials guide before planning an upgrade.

Gunsmith Level IV

The certified Frozen Trail dataset records Radial Presses, Magnetic Accelerators and Emperor Modulators as materials for Gunsmith IV. See the Gunsmith IV material checklist for exact quantities.

Do not confuse reference and owned counts

A listed cost is not evidence that your character has the material or has unlocked the room. Use the current in-game stash and progression as the source of truth when updating your tracker.
