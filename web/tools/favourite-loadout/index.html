Build around the weapons you actually want

Choose your primary and secondary weapons, then review attachment compatibility for each selected weapon. Different weapons have different attachment positions. A blank attachment position is not evidence that a part is required.

Review quick-use slots and protection

UAG’s Favourite Loadout supports six quick-use slots. Work through all positions instead of stopping after the first few, and review shield and augment choices. A saved plan is not a live inventory read: the hub does not confirm whether those items are in your current stash.

Frozen Trail weapon updates

Bantam and Stiletto were incorporated in the Frozen Trail 2.0 content pass. Bantam has no attachment slots in the recorded compatibility mapping; Stiletto uses Muzzle, Underbarrel and Stock. Do not apply a generic four-slot setup to every weapon. Read the new-weapons guide for recorded research requirements.

Recheck before every raid

After a new crafting unlock, weapon change or inventory change, review the saved selection. For the complete workflow, read the Favourite Loadout guide.
