Choose your next task

If you are missing a blueprint, start with collection tracking. If you need components, use crafting and bench planning. If you have a spare copy, review your trade offers and confirm quantities. The tools share the same companion, so you do not need separate spreadsheets for each decision.

  • Blueprint Tracker — track learned blueprints, missing entries and extra copies.
  • Trading Hub — coordinate offers using the duplicates you actually hold.
  • Crafting Planner — turn recipes into material requirements.
  • Raid Planner — choose priorities and check conditions.
  • Favourite Loadout — review equipment and attachments.
  • Bench Tracker — record workshop upgrade progress.

Use the systems together

A practical loop is: check collection → choose a target → check the items or materials needed → prepare a loadout → update your collection after the raid. Your in-game inventory is still the authority for what you physically own, and player reports do not guarantee future drops.

Public guides versus saved progress

These pages are readable without creating an account. Sign in to the UAG app to manage your personal saved progress, listings and loadouts. The companion does not connect directly to your ARC Raiders account or equip items on your behalf.
