Choose a clear target

Start with your missing blueprints or unfinished progression requirements. Select the items that matter most to you and build a short objective list. If the target depends on a bench level or crafting prerequisite, check that progression first.

Check authoritative map conditions

Map conditions can affect the opportunities available during a run. UAG references official ARC Raiders map-conditions information; check the official source when timing matters. Do not assume a screenshot from an earlier week describes a live event.

Use community reports carefully

A player report records an observation, not a guaranteed spawn or an exact drop probability. A route that worked for one squad might produce different results for yours. Review the relevant map resources and treat reports as leads to investigate.

Prepare the kit before entering

Open Favourite Loadout to review your weapons, attachments and quick-use choices. The app can record a plan but cannot equip your character, so confirm the loadout in the game before launching.
