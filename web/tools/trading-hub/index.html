What you can actually offer

A blueprint marked Owned only tells you that you have learned it. Use Dupes Owned to record physical extras you can potentially offer. Check your stash and current in-game exchange restrictions before creating a listing. UAG tracks offers and information; it does not hold items or act as an escrow service.

Agree the details before meeting

  1. Name the exact blueprint or item and the quantity each player will provide.
  2. Confirm whether the offer is an exchange or a giveaway.
  3. Check that the item can be exchanged under the game’s current rules.
  4. Record the result only after the exchange has completed in game.

Trade value is not a verified market price

Interest in an item can change following game updates, crafting changes or season resets. Estimated interest shown in companion data is not proof of in-game transferability or a real-money price. Do not give another player login codes, passwords or payments to complete an ordinary item trade.

Connect trading to collection tracking

After an exchange, return to Blueprint Tracker and correct the remaining duplicate count. For details on protecting your account and recording a failed exchange, read the safer trading guide.
