How the 91-slot collection works

The UAG Blueprint Tracker follows the established 91-slot ARC Raiders collection layout. Use the full grid overview to compare entries with your in-game collection. Mark each blueprint as owned when you have learned it, and leave unlearned blueprints in your missing list. The tracker includes zoom and scrolling controls for close inspection without changing the canonical order.

Record duplicates correctly

Learning a blueprint is not the same as holding another tradable copy. If you have learned a blueprint and found two extra copies, the record should show Owned: Yes and Dupes Owned: 2 — not three. If you trade one away, reduce the spare count to one while keeping ownership recorded.

Turn missing blueprints into a plan

Prioritise missing entries, then continue to Raid Planner. A community drop report can help you decide where to investigate but should never be treated as a guaranteed loot spawn. Check current map conditions before travelling.

Check a spare before advertising a trade

Your duplicate count can inform the Trading Hub, but it is a record you manage rather than a live read from your game account. Confirm that the extra physical copy is still in your stash before offering it to another player. The full tracking guide explains photo-import review and correcting accidental ownership changes.
