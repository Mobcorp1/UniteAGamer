Plan a recipe without guessing

Select the weapon or equipment you are considering in UAG, then review its recorded recipe and the relevant crafting station. Compare material requirements with your own stash rather than assuming everything displayed is already available. Workshop level, blueprint unlocks and materials may all be separate prerequisites.

Frozen Trail 2.0 matters

The October 2026 Frozen Trail update added Research Station progression, new weapons and components, and further Gunsmith upgrades. Some older recipes changed, including Anvil. The Frozen Trail guide explains which material groups matter and which upgrade details remain unverified.

What UAG will not invent

Certain amplification-node costs and modified repair requirements are not independently verified in the certified dataset. The planning interface should distinguish missing source data from a requirement of zero. Never commit materials based only on a placeholder cost.

Use crafting and trading together

A shortage is a planning signal, not proof that the resource can be traded. Review physical-item trade restrictions separately in Trading Hub. For bench upgrades use the Bench Tracker to maintain progression status.
