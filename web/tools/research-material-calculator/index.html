Turn the Outpost and Research Station upgrades you still need into one material checklist. Add the Bantam and Stiletto unlocks, plan a few Tier I crafts, and subtract the resources already in your stash.

Maintained by Unite A Gamer · UAG reference dataset: PASS 11D REV4, 18 October 2026 · Page created 2 November 2026

No account required. The calculator works in your browser. Your selections and entered stash amounts are stored only in this browser's local storage; nothing is uploaded to UAG. You can reset them whenever you want. It's a preparation checklist, not a live read of your ARC Raiders inventory.

      How to use this calculator without double-counting

      First select the Outpost and Research Station levels you already completed in the game. Then select a target. The checklist adds each unfinished level exactly once. If you request Research Station IV while your Outpost is only at II, the calculator includes Outpost III and IV automatically because each Research Station level has a corresponding Outpost prerequisite in UAG's reference data.

      Next choose any weapon blueprint unlocks you still need, and enter the number of Tier I weapons you actually intend to craft. The blueprint research unlock and crafting the physical weapon are separate actions: you don't spend 3,000 Research Points every time you make another Bantam. Finally, enter the quantities already in your stash. The Still need column subtracts each amount without displaying negative shortages.

      As a worked example, a fresh player aiming for Research Station I would need both Outpost I and Research Station I in this planning model. Those two levels together call for 38 Planks (3 for Outpost and 35 for Research), 5 Sheet Metal, 10 ARC Alloy, 3 Advanced Electrical Components, 5 Battered Paperbacks and 3 Mini Pumps. Enter 12 Planks as already owned and the remaining Planks requirement becomes 26. That calculation is useful before recycling or using up a resource you need elsewhere.

      For another example, selecting both Bantam and Stiletto blueprint research adds 6,000 Research Points, 8 Hornet Drivers and 15 Wasp Drivers. If Research Station I is not yet completed, its own physical material costs—and Outpost I if needed—are added separately. Crafting two Bantam I and one Stiletto I then adds 12 Simple Gun Parts and 12 Mechanical Components; it does not add more Research Points.

      What this checklist can—and cannot—tell you

      It can: aggregate recorded costs, include documented prerequisite levels, keep research currency separate from physical resources and account for material amounts you enter. You can download the current plan as a normal text file to compare with your in-game stash.

      It cannot: confirm that a room is unlocked, prove that the displayed ingredients still match the latest live patch, read your account, estimate drop rates, calculate unknown amplification node costs, or guarantee that an item can be transferred between players. The source dataset explicitly does not establish full Outpost progress persistence or prerequisite enforcement in UAG; in-game progression is authoritative. If a cost has changed, use support to report the item, level and new in-game evidence.

      Research Points are a progression currency, not a physical trading item. As an additional check, the Bantam and Stiletto research guide explains which attachment layouts differ and why a researched blueprint is not equivalent to a crafted copy.

      After building your list

      Use Crafting Planner for more recipes, Frozen Trail upgrade notes for the wider progression chain and Maps when choosing routes. This free calculator is deliberately available without registering for UAG; saving your full account-wide progression still happens in the companion app.
