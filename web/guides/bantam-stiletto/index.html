Do not use a generic attachment layout

The current UAG compatibility mapping records no attachment slots for Bantam. Stiletto uses Muzzle, Underbarrel and Stock. Check compatible items for each weapon individually in Favourite Loadout; a four-slot template would be inaccurate for both.

Blueprint research is not a per-weapon ingredient

The recorded research step unlocks a blueprint; it is not something you consume every time a weapon is crafted. Review tier-by-tier requirements in the current Crafting Planner when preparing to improve either weapon.

Calculate an unlock separately from a weapon batch

For one Bantam blueprint research unlock and two Bantam I crafts, the recorded requirements are 3,000 Research Points and 8 Hornet Drivers to research the blueprint, then 8 Simple Gun Parts and 8 Mechanical Components to craft two Tier I weapons. The research costs are not multiplied by two. If you are also unlocking Stiletto, its additional research is 3,000 Research Points and 15 Wasp Drivers.

The prerequisite Research Station level is a separate expense if you have not built it. The free Research Material Calculator calculates the Outpost and Research Station prerequisites, blueprint research and Tier I material totals together. Enter the amount already in your stash to see what is still missing. No sign-in is required; the checklist can be downloaded for use while playing.

Do not treat these recorded Tier I requirements as authoritative for higher weapon tiers, amplification, upgrades or repair. The guide reports only costs supported by the certified UAG dataset, and all actual spending should be checked against the current game.
