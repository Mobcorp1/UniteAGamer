Research Points versus physical resources

Research Points are a progression currency, not an ordinary item to list for physical trade. The certified dataset records studying Battered Paperbacks for 50 points, Raider Logbooks for 100, Exodus Technical Handbooks for 150 and ARC Data Modules for 200; Frigate Diagnostic Node is listed for 1,000 points with item treatment pending verification.

Plan research upgrades responsibly

Track your physical ingredient counts separately from earned Research Points and unlock progress. A material total does not prove the Research Station has been constructed. Outpost completion persistence and prerequisite enforcement are still incomplete in the app, so verify your room level in game before proceeding.

A worked Research Station I material plan

If you have not yet constructed either Outpost I or Research Station I, combine both stages before visiting your stash: Outpost I needs 3 Planks, 5 Sheet Metal, 10 ARC Alloy and 3 Advanced Electrical Components; Research Station I needs 35 Planks, 5 Battered Paperbacks and 3 Mini Pumps. Together that is 38 Planks plus the other recorded resources. If you already hold 12 Planks, you still need 26 for that specific plan.

For levels II–IV, always compare current completed levels against your intended goal; do not re-add stages you already finished. The free UAG Research Material Calculator performs this aggregation and lets you subtract the materials you own. Its saved figures are local to your browser and do not override your in-game progression.

For multiple blueprints, separate the Research Points needed to unlock each blueprint from the physical resources for the Research Station and subsequent weapon craft. The Bantam and Stiletto guide shows that distinction with recorded amounts.
