Where should I start?

If your missing list looks wrong, start with Blueprint tracking. A mistaken Owned flag affects the targets you see elsewhere, so correcting the collection is more useful than repeatedly refreshing the planner.

If your collection is already accurate, read Raid planning to choose a specific target and check your server region. Follow with Favourite Loadout to prepare equipment for that plan. Read Safer trading before exchanging a spare blueprint with another raider.

The articles describe the companion, rather than a guaranteed farming route. ARC Raiders changes over time, and individual raids vary. Live event conditions should be checked in the app or on the official map-conditions page instead of inferred from an old screenshot.

These guides are written by Unite A Gamer for the public UAG website. No account is needed to read them. Your saved collection and loadout stay inside the app, where you sign in to manage them.
