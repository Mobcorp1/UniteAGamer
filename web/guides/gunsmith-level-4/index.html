Check Outpost IV first

The UAG progression reference connects Gunsmith IV and Research Station IV to Outpost IV. The material count is not sufficient evidence of a completed Outpost or unlocked station. Open Outpost requirements to plan the prior upgrade.

Watch specialist resource identities

Radial Presses, Magnetic Accelerators and Emperor Modulators are different resources in the game data. Do not substitute one for another merely because they belong to a workshop category. If an icon is missing in the app, use the item name and verified inventory before applying a change.

From Gunsmith IV to amplification

The Frozen Trail reference links high-tier research and Gunsmith progression to weapon amplification. Actual node-specific material costs and certain repair requirements remain pending verification. See amplification module eligibility for the currently recorded weapon families.
