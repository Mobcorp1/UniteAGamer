Where Hand Drills are required

UseRecorded quantityOther recorded materials
Outpost III335 Planks and 3 Epoxy Buckets
Anvil I32 Advanced Mechanical Components and 3 Heavy Gun Parts

Keep the chains separate

Outpost III is a room progression expense; Anvil I is a weapon crafting recipe after the relevant blueprint unlock. Do not add them into one recipe. You may want six Hand Drills if you are planning both upgrades from a fresh material total, but that is a combined planning estimate, not the cost of either individual action.

Avoid the outdated Anvil recipe

The Frozen Trail 2.0 content pass replaced the older Anvil configuration and removed the obsolete Anvil Splitter equipable attachment. The old Splitter recycling yield is deliberately not provided as a verified number.

What to do with this information

Open the Outpost checklist and your Crafting Planner before recycling a Hand Drill. Item location and drop odds are not guaranteed by this guide.
