Progression prerequisites

The UAG reference checks Outpost level IV, Gunsmith level IV, Research Station level IV, possession of the appropriate module and weapon eligibility. Requirements vary with weapon tier and mark. The in-app checker is the relevant planning source; do not infer a valid upgrade from a module in your stash alone.

Perks change repair needs

Amplified Fragments are referenced for repair. The app data records mark-based baseline fragments, but installed perk nodes increase repair costs. There is no safe single universal repair quantity for every amplified weapon; use current game evidence for exact repair decisions.

What is not fully mapped yet

Every individual node, perk branch, modifier and exact resource amount is not yet fully verified. The guide deliberately avoids fabricated upgrade trees, guaranteed drop claims and unsupported item exchanges.
