Which progression systems changed?

The certified 18 October 2026 UAG content pass includes Outpost reference upgrades, Research Station levels I–IV, Gunsmith IV resource requirements, amplification modules Mk I–V, and the Bantam and Stiletto weapon families. The update also introduces specialist resources used by multiple systems.

How the progression connects

  1. Use the Outpost upgrade guide to identify room and project material requirements.
  2. Check the Research Station guide to plan research progression and point resources.
  3. Prepare the Gunsmith IV resources before trying higher workshop progression.
  4. Review amplification module eligibility and pending node-specific rules.
  5. Connect relevant ingredients to Crafting Planner and your saved inventory.

New material categories to watch

Important 2.0 resource groups include ARC Conductive, Insulated and Plated Couplers; Emperor Gateway Conduit; Hand Drills; Sheet Metal; Steel Cable; Laser Levels; Insulation Rolls; Pneumatic Actuators; Pressure Regulators; and Amplified Fragments. These identities are recorded separately in the certified data.

Current limitations to understand

The 11D REV4 data deliberately leaves unsupported recipe, node and modified repair quantities unresolved. Outpost cost references are present, but saved completion and prerequisite enforcement are not yet finished. A resource appearing in a trading catalogue does not establish that it can be dropped for another player.
