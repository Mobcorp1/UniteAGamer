Which materials should you keep?

Sheet Metal appears at several Outpost levels; Planks appear in both Outpost and Research Station costs. Hand Drills are used in Outpost III and the updated Anvil crafting recipe. Keeping these dependencies in view can help prevent accidental recycling of resources needed later.

Why this is a reference rather than proof of unlock

These values are taken from the certified UAG content dataset. Persistent user Outpost completion and prerequisite enforcement are not fully implemented. Check your actual in-game progress before assuming a room or workshop is available.

Combine Outpost and Research requirements before recycling

A target such as Research Station III is not simply a list of Research Station ingredients. In the certified UAG reference, level III depends on Outpost III. If your Outpost is currently at I and Research Station at I, your remaining stages for that goal are Outpost II, Outpost III, Research Station II and Research Station III. Their requirements accumulate; completed level I resources should not be counted again.

Repeated materials matter. The Outpost II cost includes 20 Sheet Metal while Outpost IV calls for another 40, but a target ending at III does not need IV yet. To see which amounts you still need, choose your completed levels in the public material calculator, set a target and enter how many materials you already hold. The result remains a checklist rather than confirmation that the upgrades have unlocked in game.
