Why coupler types must stay separate

The ARC Conductive, ARC Insulated and ARC Plated Couplers have their own canonical identities. A combined “ARC Coupler” inventory entry would hide which one is missing from a conduit requirement. Match the full name against your in-game stash before updating your planning totals.

Blueprint and availability context

The current UAG reference records the Emperor Gateway Conduit as a workbench recipe requiring its blueprint to be learned, and associates the components with the Redirection Harvester Payload activity. The exact availability of an item for player-to-player exchange must be verified separately.

Put the components into a plan

Use Crafting Planner to compare material requirements with your recorded inventory. If you cannot find one of the component names, treat that as missing information rather than substituting another material or inventing a farming guarantee.
