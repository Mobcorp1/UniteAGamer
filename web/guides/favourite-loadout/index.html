Favourite Loadout

Prepare weapons, compatible attachments and quick-use items together.

Use the loadout as a preparation record

Favourite Loadout brings your intended equipment into one place. It is useful for remembering a setup between sessions and checking that your weapons, attachments and quick-use choices make sense together.

Saving a favourite does not equip your ARC Raiders character or manufacture missing items. Compare the saved setup with your in-game inventory before entering a raid. If an item is unavailable, choose a replacement you actually have instead of assuming the companion has supplied it.

Start from the raid goal: a short collection run, a squad session and a planned exchange can call for different preparation. Review the equipment for the session you are about to play rather than carrying forward a favourite without checking it.

Select weapons before attachments

  1. Choose the primary and secondary weapons you intend to use.
  2. Review the attachment positions shown for each selected weapon.
  3. Select a compatible attachment for each position you want to fill.
  4. Check again after changing a weapon, because the compatible positions and choices can change with it.

Do not assume that every weapon has the same number of attachment slots or accepts the same attachments. UAG’s compatibility choices follow the selected weapon. A blank position can simply mean that you have not selected an item, rather than that an attachment is required.

If your preferred attachment is not available for that weapon in the picker, review compatibility in the current game. Avoid substituting an attachment purely because its name sounds similar.

Check each quick-use slot individually

The loadout includes six quick-use slots. Work through them one at a time. Consider what you want ready during the raid and whether you actually have those items available.

A useful check is to name the purpose of each selection: recovery, utility or another action in your plan. Two similar choices may be deliberate, but they should not be the result of forgetting to review the later slots.

Also review the shield and augment choices shown in the loadout. Their presence in a saved setup is a reminder to check the corresponding equipment in the game. The hub does not validate that your current character is carrying everything in the saved favourite.

PRE-RAID CHECK

  • Both weapons are the ones I intend to carry.
  • Selected attachments fit their weapons.
  • All six quick-use positions have been reviewed.
  • Shield and augment choices match the equipment I can bring.
  • The saved setup matches my actual in-game kit.

Understand the wanted-blueprint list

Favourite Loadout’s wanted-blueprint area draws from unowned blueprints that you have prioritised in your collection. It can show up to ten entries. It is a view of your collection priorities, not proof that those blueprints will drop with the selected equipment.

If an expected blueprint is absent, return to Blueprint Tracker and check its Owned flag and priority. An item marked Owned is not a wanted item in that list. If you have more prioritised missing entries than the display can show, review the ordering and focus on the ones you want next.

Use the wanted list as a reminder while planning with your squad. For timing and regional conditions, continue to Raid Planner; changing the loadout does not change the official event schedule.

Save, revisit and correct the setup

Once the selections match your intention, save the favourite and revisit it before the next session. Recheck the setup after acquiring equipment, changing weapons or consuming supplies, because the saved plan can outlast the inventory it was based on.

On a phone, rotate to landscape when you want to inspect the wider loadout arrangement. The web app uses the Android-led presentation for its equivalent landscape layout. A narrower browser still needs room to read and interact, so use the available scrolling rather than treating every item as if it must fit into one tiny viewport.

If a change appears not to save, check that you are signed in and that loading has completed. Preserve the last known setup while investigating a connection problem, and contact support with a concise description if the problem persists.

Put the guide into practice

The guide is public. Your personal collection, planner and loadout are managed in the app after signing in.
