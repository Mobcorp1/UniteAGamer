Raid planning

Turn your missing blueprints into a practical plan for your next raid.

Choose a target you can act on

A missing list tells you what you have not collected. A raid plan adds a decision: which of those items deserves your attention next? Start with a blueprint that helps an equipment goal or a trade you actually want, rather than trying to pursue every missing entry at once.

  1. Check that the blueprint is still marked unowned in Blueprint Tracker.
  2. Give it a priority and open Raid Planner under Raid & Intelligence.
  3. Put your immediate focus in the active hunt, with next-up and later targets for future sessions.
  4. Review the suggested opportunities alongside the current regional conditions.

Planner targets depend on the saved collection. An item marked Owned will no longer behave like a missing target. Correct a mistaken ownership flag before rebuilding the plan.

Check the region before the time

Regional map conditions matter because you need the information for the server region you will play in. In Raid Planner, review Regional map conditions and use Refresh regional schedule when you need an updated view.

The regional conditions service uses the official ARC Raiders map-conditions page. That page also lets you select a server region. Check it directly if the app’s conditions cannot load or a time looks unexpected.

Confirm the region and the time shown before arranging a session with a squad. A screenshot from another region or an earlier day can be misleading. If your squad is in different time zones, agree one reference time and have each person check it against their own clock.

Use the live source for live decisions.

This guide does not publish a fixed event timetable. Conditions change, and a copied timetable would quickly become less useful than the official regional view.

Read the different sources for what they establish

Official conditions establish what the official regional view currently reports. Community drop reports describe what individual players say happened in their raids. A planner suggestion connects your targets with the information available to the hub. These are different kinds of evidence.

A report that a blueprint appeared during an event does not mean that event guarantees it. A small number of reports is not enough to claim a precise drop probability. Use observations to make an informed attempt, and leave room to change the plan if the raid develops differently.

If a suggested opportunity and the live conditions do not agree, refresh the region and check the official page before committing to the timing. An unavailable feed should be treated as unavailable information, rather than filled in from memory.

Work through a practical preparation sequence

Suppose you want one missing blueprint and have a short session. First, confirm it is actually missing. Place it in your active hunt, inspect the relevant opportunities, and check which conditions apply to your server region during the time you can play.

Choose a loadout that fits your intended approach, then agree a fallback with your squad. If the desired condition is unavailable, you might pursue the next target or use the session for general supplies. That keeps the plan useful without presenting an event window as a promise of loot.

Before entering the raid, recheck the in-game map and your actual equipped items. UAG is a planning companion; it does not place you in a particular event, equip your character or reserve a drop.

Keep the plan current after a raid

Record a blueprint you acquired and adjust its extra-copy count if needed. Reopen the saved target schedule and confirm that the completed target has left your missing list. Then select the next useful goal rather than leaving a stale hunt active.

Use Clear Planner Targets when you deliberately want to replace your plan. It is a planning action, separate from correcting blueprint ownership. You do not need to clear everything after every raid.

Events and Raid Planner share the same planning destination in the current hub. Start in Raid Planner for the regional schedule and your saved targets, rather than looking for a second event timetable in Discover & Run.
