ABOUT UNITE A GAMER

A clearer view
between raids.

UAG ARC Hub is a companion for organising your own collection, planning targets, preparing equipment and coordinating with other raiders.

What we publish

This public website explains how to use those features with practical examples. The guides are original Unite A Gamer material, written around the companion’s current behaviour. You can read them without creating an account or opening the interactive app.

The app stores the progress and preferences you choose to enter. The website explains the workflow: what Owned means, how extra copies differ, how regional conditions fit into a raid plan, and what to check before a trade. The guides are designed to help you make a decision, rather than simply repeat a menu’s labels.

How we handle sources

For regional map conditions, the companion uses the official ARC Raiders map-conditions source. Player drop reports are community observations and are treated separately from official conditions. A report is not a promise of loot or evidence of a precise drop rate.

We link the live official source where timing matters instead of publishing a fixed timetable here. App walkthroughs are checked against the implementation available when they are published. Each guide shows its publication date so you can judge its context if the app or game later changes.

If you spot an incorrect label, outdated step or misleading explanation, email contact@mobcorp.co.uk with the guide URL and the specific correction. A screenshot can help show a changed interface. Do not include passwords, sign-in codes or other account secrets.

What the companion does

UAG helps you organise information. It does not read your game account, equip your character, guarantee a blueprint drop or act as escrow for an exchange. Your in-game inventory and the current game rules remain the references for what you can carry or trade.

The Android app leads the shared app presentation. The browser app uses that shared layout, including the equivalent landscape arrangement. Public guides use ordinary readable pages that adapt to a phone or a wider browser.
