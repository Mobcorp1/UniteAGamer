Map coverage in the hub

UAG’s map and Raid Intelligence systems organise points of interest and player observations across ARC Raiders locations including Blue Gate, Dam Battlegrounds, Buried City, Spaceport and Stella Montis. Map data and markers evolve, so check the actual map screen for the current available layers and locations.

Official conditions versus community intel

The official ARC Raiders map-conditions page is the timing authority used for live regional conditions. Community-submitted sightings and item drop reports are separate observations. Neither an old report nor a static map screenshot guarantees the next spawn.

Turn points into practical objectives

Start from your missing blueprints, crafting shortages or quest target, then use the map to identify which locations deserve attention. Coordinate the route with your team and take into account loadout, extraction options and uncertainty.

Reporting responsibly

When adding a report, submit only locations you actually observed and avoid presenting guesses as verified discoveries. The app should not publish personal account details or inaccurate map pins simply to make a report look complete.
